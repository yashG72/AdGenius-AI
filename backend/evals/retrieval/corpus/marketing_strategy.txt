MANO Marketing Strategy 2026

Objectives
1. Grow brand awareness among 18 to 25 year olds in the top 20 college cities.
2. Increase trial of MANO Sparkling Mango through campus sampling.
3. Build a community of 500 student creators.

Positioning
MANO is the smart alternative to colas and energy drinks: all of the fizz and fun with none of the sugar crash. The core message is "Fizz Without the Fall".

Channel plan
Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts receive 70 percent of the media budget. Campus sampling and festival sponsorships receive 20 percent. The remaining 10 percent funds creator partnerships and experiments.

Budget
The total 2026 marketing budget is 4.5 crore rupees, released quarterly.

Key dates
The summer campaign launches in April before end-of-semester exams. The monsoon limited edition launches in July. Festival season sponsorships run from September to February.
