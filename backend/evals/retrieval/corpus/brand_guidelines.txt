MANO Brand Guidelines (2026 edition)

Who we are
MANO is a premium sparkling fruit beverage made for students and young professionals who want refreshment without the sugar crash. We launched on Indian college campuses and now sell in 40 cities.

Brand personality
MANO is witty, youthful and confident. Think of MANO as the clever friend in your group chat: quick with a joke, never mean, always generous. We are playful but we are not childish, and we never punch down.

Tone of voice
Write in short, punchy sentences. Use everyday words, not marketing jargon. Light humour and self-aware jokes are welcome. Sarcasm aimed at the customer is not allowed. Avoid slang that will feel dated within a year. Emojis are allowed on social media, at most two per post.

Tagline
The official tagline is "Fizz Without the Fall". Do not modify, translate or abbreviate the tagline.

Logo usage
The MANO wordmark must always have clear space equal to the height of the letter M on every side. Never stretch, rotate or recolour the logo. On dark backgrounds use the white version of the wordmark.
