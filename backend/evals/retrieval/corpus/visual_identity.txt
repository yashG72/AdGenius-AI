MANO Visual Identity Standards

Colour palette
Primary: Mango Gold, hex #FFB300. Use it for packaging, hero backgrounds and calls to action.
Secondary: Midnight Ink, hex #1A1A1A, for body text and dark scenes.
Accent: Lime Pop, hex #9BE15D, only for limited-edition flavours and sparingly in social graphics.

Typography
Headlines use Archivo Black in uppercase. Body copy uses Inter Regular at a minimum of 14 pixels on screens.

Photography and video style
Shoot in natural daylight with warm tones. Show real students in real places such as hostels, libraries and campus festivals. Cans should appear condensation-covered and cold. Avoid studio-white backgrounds and stock photos with posed smiles.

Motion
Transitions should be quick cuts or whip pans. The signature animation is a can tab popping open with a burst of golden bubbles, used as the end card of every video.

Vertical video
For Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, frame for 9:16 and keep the product and any text inside the central safe area, away from the top 250 and bottom 340 pixels.
