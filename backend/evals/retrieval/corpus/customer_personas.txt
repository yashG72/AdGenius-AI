MANO Customer Personas

Persona 1: Campus Hustler Asha (age 20)
Second-year engineering student living in a hostel. Juggles classes, a part-time design gig and late-night group projects. Discovers products through Instagram Reels and friends. Pain point: sugary drinks leave her sluggish after lunch. Wants something fun to share at study sessions. Budget conscious; buys multipacks.

Persona 2: First-Job Rohan (age 24)
Junior analyst in Bengaluru who recently moved out of his parents' home. Watches YouTube Shorts on his commute. Wants to look put-together in front of colleagues and orders drinks for office celebrations. Values design and brands with a sense of humour.

Persona 3: Fest Organiser Meera (age 21)
Runs the cultural committee at her college. Decides which brands sponsor campus festivals. Cares about brands that engage students authentically rather than with forced corporate slogans.

Shared insights
All three personas are sceptical of advertising that feels like it is trying too hard. They respond best to humour, real student moments and creators they already follow.
