MANO Advertising Legal and Compliance Rules

Health and nutrition claims
MANO must never make health, medical or therapeutic claims. Do not describe the drink as healthy, detoxifying, immunity-boosting or a cure for anything. We may state factual nutrition information such as "4 g of sugar per can" exactly as printed on the label.

Energy and performance
Do not imply that MANO improves exam performance, concentration or athletic ability. MANO contains no added caffeine and must not be positioned as an energy drink.

Competitors
Never name or show competitor brands in advertising. Comparative claims such as "less sugar than leading colas" require prior written approval from the legal team.

Age restrictions
Advertising must not target children under 16. Do not cast actors who appear younger than 18 in paid campaigns.

Alcohol
MANO must never be shown mixed with alcohol or in bar or nightclub settings.

Approval process
All campaign scripts must be reviewed by legal at least five working days before publication.
