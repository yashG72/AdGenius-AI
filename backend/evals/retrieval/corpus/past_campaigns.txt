MANO Previous Campaigns Review

Campaign: "Crash-Free Campus" (2025)
Platform: Instagram Reels. Format: 15-second vertical videos featuring student creators pranking friends who reach for sugary colas during exam week. Results: 2.1 million views, 4.8 percent engagement rate and a 31 percent lift in campus store sales. Learning: creator-led humour outperformed polished studio ads by three times.

Campaign: "Mango Monsoon" (2025)
Platform: YouTube Shorts and outdoor billboards. Launched the Guava Chili limited edition during monsoon. Results: strong awareness but weak conversion, because billboards could not be shown near campuses. Learning: keep limited-edition launches digital-first.

Campaign: "Fest Fizz" (2024)
Sponsorship of 12 college cultural festivals with sampling booths. Results: 180,000 cans sampled, highest brand recall of any campaign. Learning: physical sampling drives trial; pair it with social content for reach.

Failed concept
A 2024 test ad joking that MANO "helps you ace exams" was withdrawn after legal review because it implied a performance claim.
