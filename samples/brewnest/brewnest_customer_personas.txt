BREWNEST CUSTOMER PERSONAS (2026 research, 1,200 survey responses)

PERSONA 1 - "Exam-Season Aditi" (38% of buyers)
Age 19-23, college student in Pune or Bengaluru. Buys Vanilla Oat from campus stores.
Drinks Brewnest during late-night study sessions and before morning lectures.
Budget-conscious: compares price per can with cafe coffee (Rs 220+). Discovers products on
Instagram Reels and from friends. Hates ads that feel "preachy" or "corporate".
Quote: "It tastes like the cafe but costs half, and I can drink it in the library."

PERSONA 2 - "First-Job Rohan" (34% of buyers)
Age 23-28, first job in tech or consulting in Hyderabad or Bengaluru. Buys Original in
6-packs online. Drinks it on the 7 am commute (as a passenger) and at his desk.
Values efficiency and quality; follows coffee creators on YouTube.
Quote: "I don't have 20 minutes to queue at a cafe before standup."

PERSONA 3 - "Weekend Host Meera" (17% of buyers)
Age 28-35, works from home, buys the 1 litre concentrate to make drinks for friends.
Loves recipes and the Mango Tonic. Active on Pinterest and Instagram.
Quote: "Mango tonic with Brewnest is my party trick."

KEY INSIGHTS
- 71% of buyers first tried Brewnest because a friend shared a can.
- Top purchase reasons: taste (64%), price vs cafes (52%), convenience (47%).
- Main barrier: "I didn't know cold brew is less bitter than iced coffee" (29%).
